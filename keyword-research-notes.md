# Keyword Research Notes

## Settings used
- **Google Keyword Planner** → Discover new keywords → Location: India · Language: English · Search network: Google
- **Google Trends** → Region: India · Time range: Past 12 months · Category: Food & Drink

## Seed keywords
`filter coffee` · `coffee beans` · `coffee maker` · `black coffee` · `coffee subscription` · `bitter coffee`

## How search intent was decided
| Intent | Signal words | What page 1 shows | Content format chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informational | how to, why, what is, benefits | Recipes, guides, Wikipedia-style articles | How-to / explainer |
| Commercial investigation | best, vs, review, compare | Listicles, comparison posts | Buying guide / comparison |
| Transactional | buy, online, subscription, price | Product & category pages | Checklist + product-led post |

## Selection criteria for target keywords
1. Directly connected to a product BrewNest sells
2. Matches a real audience pain point
3. Has meaningful monthly searches in India
4. Realistic to rank for a new blog (prefer long-tail / question keywords)

## Google Trends observations
- `coffee beans online`, `best coffee beans in india`, `coffee subscription` → rise from October, peak around Diwali and December (gifting)
- `black coffee benefits` → rising interest (health & fitness)
- `how to make filter coffee`, `arabica vs robusta`, `how to store coffee beans` → stable all year (evergreen)

**Scheduling decision:** evergreen awareness posts in weeks 1–2, buying-intent posts in weeks 3–4 to catch the seasonal rise.
