# ☕ Blog Content Plan — BrewNest Coffee

> **Digital Marketing Track · Task: Create a Blog Content Plan**
> A one-month (November 2026) SEO blog strategy built from keyword research and audience pain points.

**Author:** Reddi Malli Nandini
**Tools:** Google Sheets · Google Keyword Planner · Google Trends

---

## 📌 Objective

Learn how **SEO and content strategy work together** by planning a month of blog posts where every topic is backed by:

- a **target keyword** people actually search for (Keyword Planner),
- the **timing** of that demand (Google Trends),
- a real **audience pain point**, and
- a clear place in the **customer journey** (Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Retention).

## 🏢 Business & Audience

**Business:** A (fictional) direct-to-consumer specialty coffee brand based in Hyderabad that sells freshly roasted Indian single-origin beans, South Indian filter-coffee blends, brewing gear and a monthly subscription online.

**Target audience:** Urban Indians aged 22-40 (students, young professionals, work-from-home folks) who drink instant coffee or cafe coffee and want to make better coffee at home without spending a lot.

## 😣 Audience Pain Points

| Pain point | Detail | Posts that solve it |
|---|---|---|
| Home coffee tastes bitter or flat | Bitterness/sourness from wrong grind, water temperature or stale beans | #1, #2 |
| Confused by bean types and roasts | Arabica vs Robusta, light vs dark roast, chicory or no chicory | #3, #5 |
| Don't know which equipment to buy | French press, pour-over, moka pot or South Indian filter - which one suits them | #6 |
| Cafe coffee is expensive daily | Rs 250+ per cup adds up; want cafe quality at home | #4, #7, #9 |
| Unsure if buying beans online is worth it | Freshness, roast date, delivery time, trust in new brands | #8 |
| Beans go stale quickly | Don't know how to store coffee or how long it lasts | #10 |

## 🧭 Customer Journey Coverage

| Stage | Posts | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | 4 (40%) | Attract new readers through problems and questions |
| Consideration | 3 (30%) | Help readers compare options and products |
| Decision | 2 (20%) | Remove doubts and drive the first order |
| Retention | 1 (10%) | Keep customers happy and buying again |

**Search intent mix:** 5 Informational · 3 Commercial investigation · 2 Transactional

## 🔍 Methodology

1. **Defined the business and audience** — who buys, what they drink now, what stops them from buying.
2. **Collected pain points** from Google "People also ask", Reddit/Quora coffee threads and product reviews.
3. **Keyword research** in Google Keyword Planner (Location: India, Language: English) using seed terms like *filter coffee, coffee beans, coffee maker, black coffee*; chose one primary + three secondary keywords per topic.
4. **Checked seasonality** in Google Trends — buying/gifting queries rise Oct–Jan, so decision-stage posts are scheduled in the last two weeks of November.
5. **Classified search intent** by checking what ranks on page 1 for each keyword.
6. **Mapped topics to the funnel** and linked them internally so a reader can go from *"why is my coffee bitter"* → brewer comparison → purchase.
7. **Built the calendar in Google Sheets** with a status dropdown and a funnel summary tab (COUNTIF/SUMIF formulas).

## 💡 Key Learnings

- **Keywords + pain points = content that is both found and useful.** One without the other fails.
- **Search intent decides the format** — "how to" → guide, "vs" → comparison, "buy" → trust checklist.
- **Funnel mapping** turns a list of blogs into a path that ends in a sale.
- **Google Trends adds timing** — publish buying-intent posts before seasonal peaks.
- **A structured sheet** (objective, CTA, status) makes the strategy measurable and easy to hand to writers.
